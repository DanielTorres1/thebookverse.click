Book Overview

In Why We Get Sick, leading scientist and bio-molecular researcher Dr. Benjamin Bikman presents a groundbreaking analysis of the single most pervasive yet unrecognized health crisis of our time: insulin resistance.

While modern medicine often treats conditions like high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, obesity, fatty liver disease, cardiovascular disease, and dementia as separate illnesses, Dr. Bikman demonstrates that they share a common root. By understanding how insulin impacts every cell in the body, readers gain the power to prevent and even reverse chronic degeneration.

Unlike fad diet books, Why We Get Sick translates rigorous scientific literature into clear, actionable advice that anyone can implement immediately.

Key Takeaways & Practical Rules

Dr. Bikman outlines simple dietary and lifestyle guidelines to restore insulin sensitivity:

  • 1. Control Carbohydrates: Limit processed sugars and refined grains that spike glucose and keep insulin elevated.
  • 2. Prioritize Protein: Ensure adequate high-quality protein to maintain muscle mass and satiety without elevating insulin unnecessarily.
  • 3. Don't Fear Natural Fats: Healthy fats (olive oil, avocados, butter) have minimal impact on insulin levels.
  • 4. Mind the Frequency (Fasting): Give your digestive system breaks with intermittent fasting to allow baseline insulin levels to fall.
  • 5. Exercise for Insulin Sensitivity: Incorporate resistance training and movement after meals to absorb glucose without relying heavily on insulin.
